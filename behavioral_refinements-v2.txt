[ADS-REQ-001A] Ego Hazard Lights Activation on Breakdown or Accident
  Source Framework: UN Vienna Convention on Road Traffic (1968)
  Applicability Area: Universal
  Category: Vehicle Lighting & Signaling | Sub-Category: Hazard Warning Lights
  Statutory / Standard Ref: Article 32, Paragraph 13(a) & Article 7, Paragraph 2
  Triggering Conditions / Context: Vehicle breakdown, Accident occurrence
  Behavioral Expectation Text: The ADS must immediately activate its hazard warning signal (simultaneous flashing direction-indicators) if the vehicle breaks down or is involved in an accident and cannot be moved immediately, to warn other road-users of the obstacle it constitutes.
  Required Meta-Action Sequence:
    1. [ACT-03] Decelerate (Implement longitudinal deceleration)
    2. [ACT-13] Move Out of Travel Lane/Park (Exit active lane onto shoulder or safe stop zone)
    3. [ACT-04] Stop (Achieve complete safe stop)
    4. [ACT-15] Transition to MRC: ADS Implements Minimal Risk Maneuver (Maintain safe standstill state)

[ADS-REQ-003A] Ego Beam-Switching to Prevent Dazzling
  Source Framework: UN Vienna Convention on Road Traffic (1968)
  Applicability Area: Universal
  Category: Vehicle Lighting & Signaling | Sub-Category: Headlamps
  Statutory / Standard Ref: Article 32, Paragraph 2
  Triggering Conditions / Context: Nightfall, Dawn, Adequately lit road, Close following
  Behavioral Expectation Text: The ADS must switch from driving lamps (high-beams) to passing lamps (low-beams) immediately when passing oncoming traffic, when following closely behind another vehicle, or when driving on an adequately lit roadway, to avoid dazzling other road-users.
  Required Meta-Action Sequence:
    1. [ACT-01] Follow Vehicle (Detect oncoming driver within 500ft or lead vehicle within 300ft)
    2. [ACT-03] Decelerate (Adjust speed for lower illumination range)
    3. [ACT-11] Shift Within Lane (Maintain center lane tracking with low-beam passing lamps active)
