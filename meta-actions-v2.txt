================================================================================
META-ACTIONS / CONTROL ACTIONS SPECIFICATION (v2.0)
Source: "A Framework for Automated Driving System Testable Cases and Scenarios"
        (USDOT / NHTSA Report DOT HS 812 623, Page 58) + Directional Extension
================================================================================

[ACT-01] Follow Vehicle
  Category: Longitudinal & Lateral Control
  Description: Implement lateral and/or longitudinal control actions to maintain a safe following distance from an immediate lead vehicle, while continuing to follow the current lane of travel.

[ACT-02] Accelerate
  Category: Longitudinal Control
  Description: Implement longitudinal control actions to increase speed, as appropriate and lawful.

[ACT-03] Decelerate
  Category: Longitudinal Control
  Description: Implement longitudinal control actions to decrease speed, as appropriate.

[ACT-04] Stop
  Category: Longitudinal Control
  Description: Implement longitudinal control actions to decelerate in a safe and stable manner to a complete stop.

[ACT-05] Yield
  Category: Right-of-Way & Deceleration
  Description: Relinquish right-of-way to another road user, including decelerating or stopping as required by traffic regulations or safety etiquette.

[ACT-06] Change Lane
  Category: Lateral & Longitudinal Maneuver
  Description: Implement longitudinal and/or lateral control actions to shift into an adjacent lane.

[ACT-07] Abort Lane Change
  Category: Lateral & Longitudinal Maneuver
  Description: Cancel the maneuver to shift into an adjacent lane (remain in or return to original lane).

[ACT-08] Pass
  Category: Lateral & Longitudinal Maneuver
  Description: Implement longitudinal and/or lateral control actions to shift into an adjacent lane to accelerate to desired speed and overtake another vehicle.

[ACT-09] Abort Pass
  Category: Lateral & Longitudinal Maneuver
  Description: Cancel maneuver to shift into an adjacent lane (remain in or return to original lane).

[ACT-10] Turn
  Category: Lateral & Longitudinal Maneuver
  Description: Implement lateral and longitudinal control actions to transition from current road/lane to connecting road/lane (includes parameterizations for left, right, and U-turns).

[ACT-11] Shift Within Lane
  Category: Lateral Control / Nudge
  Description: Implement lateral and/or longitudinal control actions such that the ADS does not follow the center (or near-center) of the current lane but remains fully within the current lane.

[ACT-12] Shift Outside of Lane
  Category: Lateral Control / Encroachment
  Description: Implement lateral and/or longitudinal control actions such that the ADS partially or fully moves outside of the current lane of travel (i.e., one or more wheels cross the lane boundary).

[ACT-13] Move Out of Travel Lane/Park
  Category: Egress & Parking Maneuver
  Description: Implement lateral and longitudinal control actions such that the ADS fully exits the current active lane of travel onto a shoulder or parking lane and stops.

[ACT-14] Transition to MRC: Return Control to Fallback-Ready User
  Category: System Fallback & Handover
  Description: Return longitudinal and lateral control to human occupant/driver (while providing sufficient advance warning).

[ACT-15] Transition to MRC: ADS Implements Minimal Risk Maneuver
  Category: System Fallback & Autonomous MRC
  Description: Implement lateral and/or longitudinal control actions to achieve a minimal risk condition automatically without reliance on human driver takeover.

[ACT-16] Reverse / Back Up
  Category: Longitudinal & Directional Control
  Description: Implement reverse gear longitudinal and lateral control actions to move the vehicle backward safely while monitoring rear blind-spot hazards (grounded in Vienna Convention Art. 14/25, Texas Code Sec. 545.415, and NHTSA Pre-Crash Scenarios 8, 15, 33-35).
